End user guide

Signing in, two-factor authentication, your account and signing out, for people who use services protected by Symbolon Gateway and Symbolon ID

This chapter is for the people who use the services. It explains, in plain language, the sign-in screen you see when you open a work system, how to set up an authenticator app, how to change your password, what the error screens mean, and how to sign out.

Once you sign in with your organization account, you can open other services connected the same way without signing in again. Creating accounts and resetting a lost authenticator app are handled by your organization's account administrator.

  • Signing inHow to use the Symbolon ID sign-in screen that appears when you open a work service, and what to check when you can't sign in
  • Set up two-factor authenticationHow to register an authenticator app with Symbolon ID, enter a verification code when you sign in, and keep your recovery codes
  • My accountHow to change your password and manage two-factor authentication and recovery codes on the Symbolon ID My account screen
  • Access denied and temporary outage screensWhat the access denied (403) and can't-reach-sign-in-service (503) screens that appear instead of a service mean, and what to do
  • Signing outHow to sign out of a service and Symbolon ID, the sign-out confirmation screen, and how signing out carries over to other services
