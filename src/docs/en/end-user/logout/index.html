Signing out

How to sign out of a service and Symbolon ID, the sign-out confirmation screen, and how signing out carries over to other services

On this page

  • Other services sign you out too
  • The "Are you sure you want to sign out?" page

This page explains how to sign out, and how signing out in one place also signs you out of other services.

Sign out#

Sign out in one of these ways:

  • Click the sign-out menu in the service (if the service has one).
  • Click 로그아웃 (Sign out) on the My account screen.

When you sign out, Symbolon ID asks you to confirm.

  1. Check that the account named in the message is yours.
  2. Click 로그아웃 (Sign out).

Some services finish right away without this confirmation screen. When signing out is done, you go back to the service's starting address. Because you're no longer signed in, the sign-in screen appears again. If there's nowhere to go back to, you end on a "로그아웃되었습니다" (You've been signed out) message.

Other services sign you out too#

When you sign out of Symbolon ID, you're also signed out of the other services you opened with that sign-in. This usually takes effect within a few seconds. In any other service tab that's still open, you'll be asked to sign in again when you move to the next page.

On a public computer or a device you share with others, always sign out and close the browser when you're done.

Note

Closing the browser alone may not end your Symbolon ID sign-in. On shared devices, always click the 로그아웃 (Sign out) button.

The "Are you sure you want to sign out?" page#

If you reach a service's sign-out address by clicking a link in an email or on another site, you aren't signed out right away. You see this confirmation page instead. It stops other sites from quietly signing you out.

  • If you really want to sign out, click 로그아웃 (Sign out).
  • If you didn't mean to sign out, just close the window or go to the service address. You stay signed in.

If you click the sign-out menu inside the service, or type the address in the address bar yourself, you're signed out right away without this page.

When an administrator signs you out#

If your account administrator ends your sign-in, or resets your password or 2FA, you're signed out of all services. When you change your password or set up 2FA yourself, you're also signed out on every device except the browser you're using. If you then see "이 계정의 세션이 관리자에 의해 종료되었습니다" (An administrator ended this account's session), wait a moment and sign in again (Access denied and temporary outage screens).

For how this works on the administrator side, see Sessions and Back-Channel Logout and immediate revocation.
