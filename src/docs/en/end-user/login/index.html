Signing in

How to use the Symbolon ID sign-in screen that appears when you open a work service, and what to check when you can't sign in

  • Sign in

  • If you can't sign in

When you open a work service, the sign-in screen appears first. This page explains how to sign in and what to check when you can't.

Sign in#

  1. Go to the service address as usual (for example, https://wiki.corp.example).
  2. If you aren't signed in yet, you're taken to the Symbolon ID sign-in screen automatically.
  3. Check that the domain in the address bar is your organization's sign-in address.
  4. Enter your 사용자 이름 (Username) and 비밀번호 (Password), then click 로그인 (Sign in).
  5. If the service asks for extra verification, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app (Two-factor authentication).
  6. You're returned to the page you were trying to open.

Warning

As the note at the bottom of the sign-in screen says, always check the domain in the address bar before you enter your password. A link you got by email or messenger could be phishing that sends you to a look-alike address. Ask your account administrator for your organization's sign-in address.

Use 라이트 · 시스템 설정 · 다크 (Light · System · Dark) in the top-right corner to choose the screen theme.

Sign in once, use other services too#

After you sign in once, for a while you won't see the sign-in screen again when you open other services connected to the same account. The screen may flicker briefly as it passes through. That's normal.

SituationWhat happens
You keep using a serviceYou stay signed in. After a set time per service, your sign-in is checked again in the background, with no screen
You haven't used any service for more than 8 hoursYou need to sign in again
It's been 24 hours since you signed inYou need to sign in again

The exact times can differ depending on your organization's settings.

If you can't sign in#

Message on screenWhat it means and what to do
"사용자 이름 또는 비밀번호가 올바르지 않습니다." (The username or password is incorrect.)Check your username and password again. Also check Caps Lock and the Korean/English input mode
"여러 번 틀리면 같은 접속 위치에서의 로그인이 잠시 지연됩니다. 올바른 비밀번호도 거절되면 조금 뒤에 다시 시도하세요." (After several wrong attempts, sign-ins from the same location are briefly delayed. If even the correct password is rejected, try again a little later.)You may be briefly delayed after several wrong attempts. Wait a few minutes and try again. If it still doesn't work, ask your account administrator to unlock your account
"로그인 시도 횟수가 너무 많습니다. 잠시 후 다시 시도하세요." (Too many sign-in attempts. Try again later.)There were many failures from the same location. Wait a few minutes and try again
"지금은 요청이 많아 처리할 수 없습니다. 잠시 후 다시 시도하세요." (Too many requests right now. Try again later.)Lots of people are signing in at once. Try again in a few seconds
"요청을 확인할 수 없습니다. 페이지를 새로 고친 뒤 다시 시도하세요." (The request couldn't be verified. Refresh the page and try again.)The screen was open for a long time, or your sign-in status changed in another tab. Refresh and try again

Note

For security, the sign-in screen doesn't tell you whether the password was wrong, the account doesn't exist, or the account is suspended. If your password is correct but keeps being rejected, contact your account administrator.

If you signed in but see "접근 권한이 없습니다" (You don't have access) or "로그인 서비스에 연결할 수 없습니다" (Can't connect to the sign-in service) instead of the service, see Access denied and temporary outage screens.

If you forgot your password#

Symbolon ID has no self-service password recovery. Ask your account administrator to reset your password. After a reset you're signed out on all devices, so sign in again with the new password. Once you're signed in, change it to a password of your choice in My account.
