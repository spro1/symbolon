Set up two-factor authentication

How to register an authenticator app with Symbolon ID, enter a verification code when you sign in, and keep your recovery codes

  • Set it up
  • Keep your recovery codes
  • Enter a code when you sign in

This page explains how to set up an authenticator app on your phone and how to keep your recovery codes. With two-factor authentication (2FA), it's hard for anyone else to sign in to your account even if your password leaks.

What you need#

  • An authenticator app on your phone, such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator. Any app that makes 6-digit time-based codes (TOTP) works.
  • Your current password

Set it up#

You'll see the setup screen in two cases. It opens automatically the first time you open a service that requires 2FA, and you can also open it yourself by clicking 2단계 인증 등록 (Set up 2FA) in My account.

  1. Open your authenticator app, tap add account (＋), then choose "Enter a setup key" (some apps call it "Enter key manually").
  2. Type the 설정 키 (Setup key) from the screen into the app. Give the account a name you'll recognize (for example, "Symbolon ID") and choose "Time-based".
    • If you're viewing this screen on your phone, you can also expand otpauth 주소로 등록 (Set up with otpauth link) and tap the link.
  3. Enter your password in 현재 비밀번호 (Current password) on the setup screen.
  4. Enter the 6-digit number shown in your authenticator app in 인증 코드 (Verification code), then click 등록 후 계속 (Set up and continue).
  5. You'll see 8 복구 코드 (Recovery codes). Store them as described in the next section, then click 보관 확인 후 계속 (I've stored them, continue).

When setup is done, you're signed out on every other device except this browser. Sign in again on those devices.

Note

The Symbolon ID setup screen has no QR code. You type the setup key in yourself. Each time you open the setup screen, a new setup key is made. If you reopened the screen before finishing, add the new key to your app again (you'll see "설정 키가 새로 발급되었습니다" (A new setup key was issued)).

Caution

Anyone who has the setup key can make your verification codes. Don't take a screenshot of it, don't save it in notes or messengers, and don't show it to anyone. If you saved it by mistake, tell your account administrator and set it up again.

Tip

Administrator accounts that use the admin console must also enter an 등록 코드 (Enrollment code) the first time they set up 2FA. It's a one-time code you get from the person who created your account, through a different channel than your password (First enrollment for administrators).

Keep your recovery codes#

Recovery codes are one-time codes you enter instead of a verification code when you've lost your phone or can't use your authenticator app. There are 8 of them, and they look like abcde-fghij.

  • They're shown only this once. You can't see them again.
  • Keep them somewhere other than your phone. For example, in a password manager, or written on paper in a locked drawer.
  • Each code works only once. You can see how many you have left in My account.
  • If you've used almost all of them, or someone else may have seen them, click 복구 코드 재발급 (Reissue recovery codes) in My account. All your old codes stop working.

Enter a code when you sign in#

When you open a service that requires 2FA, this screen appears after your password. If you're already signed in with your password and move to a page that requires 2FA, you won't be asked for your password again. You'll see only this screen.

  1. Open your authenticator app and find the 6-digit number for Symbolon ID.
  2. Enter it in 인증 코드 (Verification code) and click 확인 (Confirm). The number changes every 30 seconds, so if it's about to change, wait for the next one.
  3. If you can't use your authenticator app, enter a recovery code in the same box.

If you keep seeing "인증 코드가 올바르지 않습니다" (The verification code is incorrect), check that your phone's clock is set automatically. After several wrong tries, codes aren't accepted for a while.

If you change or lose your phone#

SituationWhat to do
You got a new phone (and still have the old one)Use your authenticator app's account transfer feature, or sign in with a recovery code and ask your account administrator for a reset so you can set it up again on the new phone
You lost your phone but have recovery codesSign in with a recovery code. Then tell your account administrator so they can reset your 2FA, and set it up again
You have neither your phone nor recovery codesAsk your account administrator to reset your 2FA. After they confirm who you are and reset it, you're signed out on all devices and set it up again the next time you sign in
