My account

How to change your password and manage two-factor authentication and recovery codes on the Symbolon ID My account screen

On this page
  • Open My account
  • Change your password
  • Manage two-factor authentication

This page explains how to change your password and check your two-factor authentication status on the 내 계정 (My account) screen.

Open My account#

In your browser, open your organization's Symbolon ID address with /account added to the end (for example, https://sso.corp.example/account). If you aren't signed in, you'll see the sign-in screen first. Signing in directly at the Symbolon ID address also brings you to this screen.

ItemWhat it means
사용자 이름 (Username), 이름 (Name), 이메일 (Email)Your account details. To change them, ask your account administrator
인증 수준 (Authentication level)Whether your current sign-in used only a 비밀번호 (Password), or 비밀번호 + 2단계 인증 (Password + 2FA)
2단계 인증(TOTP) (Two-factor authentication)등록됨 (Enrolled) or 미등록 (Not enrolled)
복구 코드 (Recovery codes)How many recovery codes you have left (only if you've set up 2FA)

Below these you'll find the 비밀번호 변경 (Change password), 2단계 인증 등록 (Set up 2FA, shown when not enrolled), 복구 코드 재발급 (Reissue recovery codes, shown when enrolled) and 로그아웃 (Sign out) buttons. Symbolon ID administrators also see a 관리 콘솔 (Admin console) button.

Change your password#

  1. Click 비밀번호 변경 (Change password).
  2. Enter your 현재 비밀번호 (Current password).
  3. Enter the same value in 새 비밀번호 (New password) and 새 비밀번호 확인 (Confirm new password).
  4. Click 변경 (Change).

Your new password must meet these rules:

  • At least 12 characters (this can differ by organization setting; the screen shows the rule)
  • It can't contain your username
  • Commonly used passwords aren't allowed

After you change it, you're signed out on every other device except this browser. On your phone or other computers, sign in again with the new password. If you think someone else knows your password, change it right away. That also signs them out.

Note

If you enter your current password wrong several times, you'll be briefly delayed, the same way as on the sign-in screen. If you've forgotten your current password, ask your account administrator to reset it.

Manage two-factor authentication#

  • If it says 미등록 (Not enrolled), click 2단계 인증 등록 (Set up 2FA) to set it up (Set up two-factor authentication).
  • If it says 등록됨 (Enrolled), check how many recovery codes you have left. If you're running low, click 복구 코드 재발급 (Reissue recovery codes). You'll see 8 new codes once, and all your old codes stop working.
  • You can reissue recovery codes only when you signed in with 2FA (인증 수준 (Authentication level) shows 비밀번호 + 2단계 인증 (Password + 2FA)). If you signed in with just your password, sign out and come back in through a service that requires 2FA, or contact your account administrator.
  • If you changed or lost your authenticator app, ask your account administrator to reset your 2FA. You can't remove an enrolled authenticator app yourself.
